devglow presets

Palette data: devglow @ 5c131e1a3d (MIT). Left: devglow's own dark colors. Then our dark with the neutral's step-9 chroma capped at 0.03 (current default) and at 0.06 (proposed, option darkChromaCap), then our light. "fit ΔE" = mean OKLab distance from devglow's 5 surface colors to the nearest of our 8 dark surface steps (below ~0.02 is not visible). Mean over 12 palettes: cap 0.03 → 0.0123, cap 0.06 → 0.0111.
